Hi!

I currently lead various internal research initiatives at F-Prime. I broadly studied Computational and Applied Mathematics at Stanford University.

Previously, I worked in Proprietary Research at Point72 and did Research Engineering stints at Amgen and Moderna, where I worked on machine learning applications in R&D and wet lab automation. I also had a stint as a summer associate at a consulting firm where I worked on biopharma pricing. My research experience ranges from molecular language modeling to educational econometrics to algorithmic fairness.

I find fullfillment in ideating solutions for systems that run society. Outside of that, you might catch me poring over an atlas or scrolling through linguistics forums.
